Event Guide

A splash show synced to energetic tunes and bright colors.

Safety Notes

Every guest should check in at Welcome Point before heading into the festival.

Hold on to your belongings near the Fountain Stage after 10pm.

Action Commands

Start the ride: stage.start("Skyline Ride")

Light the path: lantern.ignite("Lantern Walk")

Pause the show: water.pause("Water Beat")
