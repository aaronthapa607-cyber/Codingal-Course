My School Clubs

Mathcircle

A club where you work in teams to solve math questions. It's fun when I work those questions out with my friends.

Chess Club

A club where you get to learn chess and play it against other people. I find it fun because you have to think hard for your next move and you can play against your friends.

Math Olympiad

A club where you take a five question test where each question is very hard. I like it because it challenges me and encourages me to think outside the box.
